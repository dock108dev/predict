# Local development workflow

The owner requires one working copy: this repository, on `main`.

- Implement, integrate, build and run the app here. Do not create isolated
  successor repositories, candidate workspaces or worktrees unless the owner
  explicitly requests one.
- Routine local integration and restart belong to normal development; do not
  defer completed changes behind a separate installation or release slice.
- Engineers have full local access to complete their assigned outcome. Change
  any necessary files, interfaces or tests and make routine implementation
  decisions without additional permission or per-file approval.
- Build, integrate and restart both app and data processes as needed, including
  normal acquisition under the existing configuration and budgets. Preserve
  explicit owner Stop choices and account/quota/hold records.
- Full engineering access includes API use and paid collection needed for the
  selected product scope. Do not ask again for API or acquisition approval.
  Execute within the recorded credit/account limits and preserve actual Stop
  choices and unresolved financial exposure. The six-league, three-family
  aggregate scope is authorized up to 18 credits per 15-minute cycle and 1,008
  credits per operating day through October 31, 2026 at 8 PM Eastern, subject to
  the existing account-window, quota and hold guards.
- Use proportionate verification for the behavior changed. Handoffs explain the
  outcome, relevant evidence and remaining issues; they do not impose arbitrary
  diagnostic-attempt limits, mandatory receipt bundles or approval addenda.
- Use disposable fixtures/databases for tests without cloning the repository.
- Preserve existing uncommitted work, credentials, settings, user data and
  account/quota/hold records. Do not reset them to simplify development.
- Old candidate manifests and closeouts are historical evidence. Their former
  isolation/handover instructions are superseded by this workflow.
- Recoverable old versions are archived under `.local/dev-consolidation-20261016/`.
  They are not active development checkouts. Do not recreate them for new work.
