Predict models a new standalone position placed directly with the venue, taking the posted price and holding to settlement.

The preview uses the high end of the applicable standard taker schedule. Upfront fees count against your budget. A winning commission comes out of your payout.

Kalshi

Pay when your order fills. The exchange fee depends on contract price, quantity, and the applicable market multiplier. Predict uses the higher documented exchange rounding case.

Access through an intermediary can carry separate charges.

Official Kalshi fee schedule

Polymarket US

Pay when your taker order fills. The fee depends on the price, contract quantity, and the tier set when the order is entered. Predict uses the highest applicable standard tier and assumes zero rebates.

Order rounding and caps can change the effective charge at different trade sizes.

Official Polymarket US fee schedule

Novig

Eligible pregame straight trades have no taker fee. Predict verifies the exact market and its pregame status before showing a zero fee. In-game, futures, and parlay terms can differ.

Official Novig fee rules

ProphetX

NHL straight trades can incur a fee at each fill. Other supported straight markets charge commission on positive net gains at settlement.

Fill prices and carry-forward rounding affect NHL charges. For settlement commission, other positions in the same market can change net gains. The preview models one standalone position.
