Sizing, recording and result assumptions

At 4% EV, −110 commits $110, +300 commits about $33.33, and +3000 about $3.33 before extra fees. Kelly uses the recorded probability and net cashflows; each sized ticket is repriced with its captured fee assumptions and must still clear 4%. Quantity grids can reduce actual commitment.

Tickets are reproducible replays of immutable T−10 receipts, not claims of orders entered at T−10. Repeated reads produce the same ticket IDs. Both portfolios share the same observations; do not add their totals together.

The model assumes unlimited size at the recorded price. Sporting results can model decisive wins and losses. Pushes, voids and unverified schedule changes remain unresolved. Peak commitment uses recorded result availability; unresolved tickets keep their cash tied up. No depth, execution or venue settlement is established.

Use the date control to replay another day with this versioned policy as evidence accumulates. Missing captures are never replaced with early observations, later prices or outcomes.
