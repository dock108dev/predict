How coverage is counted

Each native price observation counts once in its sport, source, family and period cell. A supported peer is an equivalent captured observation from another source. A current peer also passes the original clock and publication checks.

One underlying pair counts once globally, even when both sources show it. Source-cell pair totals overlap.

Discovery counts describe the exact captured query and may cover a different window from retained prices. Gap causes may overlap. Primary counts reconcile affected observations.
