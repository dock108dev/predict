What the measurements mean

Each native price observation counts once in its sport, source, family and period cell. A supported peer is an equivalent captured observation from another source; a current peer also passes the original clock and publication checks. Optional return estimates do not determine price or comparison availability.

Canonical outcomes, underlying cross-source pairs and signed comparison directions have separate units. One underlying pair counts once globally, even when both sources show it. Source-cell pair totals overlap.

Discovery counts describe the exact captured query. Bounded or partial listings do not measure a provider’s entire catalog, and may describe a different window from retained prices. Unknown remains unknown. Gap causes may overlap; primary counts reconcile affected observations.

Filters and drilldowns read this captured local publication. They never dispatch a provider request. Background collection and publication continue independently.
