November 5, 2026 Eastern. Public-site straight contracts version 1.0, linked from https://www.prophetx.co/lobby/t-c/trading-fees. NHL takers pay a per-contract fee of 0.035 times price times (1 minus price), rounded half up to cents per fill with an order remainder. Other leagues use 2% of positive net market gains. The normalized estimate assumes one fill on a new order and a single position. Actual account terms require independent evidence.
