This does not establish separately assigned API/account terms; https://docs.prophetx.co/docs/fees explicitly distinguishes account-assigned commission and maker/taker schedules and has no current-rate query endpoint.

A one-position-per-market public-site scenario uses positive net market profit, cent-ceiling commission as an explicit conservative rounding assumption, and no offsetting holdings.
