## Acceptance policy

The user must be able to open Predict, understand useful same-selection price differences and inspect them before trading manually elsewhere. Acceptance requires a readable automatic workflow, not a percentage of market coverage. The 63 requirements and 252 source records remain factual accounting and the broader roadmap.

| Workflow | Required behavior for the replacement product |
| --- | --- |
| Open | The configured app owns data flow. Ordinary users see current prices or an honest connecting/unavailable state without Start/Stop/Refresh, duration, saved-scan or setup tasks. No historical/test fallback masquerades as current data. |
| Compare | Compact grouped events, exact outcomes/periods/lines and aligned venue prices. Both American odds and cents are visible; native cents and converted equivalents are distinguishable. Better quoted price is not a guaranteed profit or EV claim. |
| Narrow | League/sport, market, applicable period, venue and game search consistently filter available data. Filtering does not create duplicate acquisition or paid requests. Empty states are useful and recoverable. |
| Inspect | Understandable Details holds the chosen price revision coherently, with native price/conversion, relevant age/state/rules/costs and supported calculations or concise unavailable reasons. Expired ephemeral selections are explicit. No permanent replay guarantee is required. |
| Follow updates | Shared automatic updates keep identity, reading position and focus stable. Native and slower budgeted aggregate ages/cadences are honest; stale/disconnected/unknown data never becomes fresh because a browser connection is active. |
| Operate | Separate owner/admin controls expose source health, metrics, quota, shutdown and source issues. Native acquisition/recovery and aggregate spending are bounded. New ordinary operation has bounded latest-state storage and no ongoing quote-history requirement. |
| Manual decision | Raw price differences, arbitrage return, supported EV and manual What-if have separate meaning. Orders remain outside Predict. Negative, zero and unavailable results are valid. |

Kalshi and Polymarket US remain native; Novig and ProphetX share The Odds API. All four remain intended participants; simultaneous availability everywhere is unnecessary. Full-game winner/spread/total take priority. Native cadence must be verified. Aggregate cadence may be slower to respect the owner-reported 500/month shared allowance, with visible ages and dependent-calculation eligibility. Seasonal/niche gaps do not automatically block useful supported comparisons.

## Calculations, storage and evidence boundaries

Unknown account costs, settlement cashflows, quantity/depth or units withhold only dependent outputs. EV requires a supported probability with disclosed basis. Deterministic scenario analysis does not require a probability. References from Pinnacle/DraftKings/BetMGM remain references. Informational depth is not a fill guarantee. Dual-format quotes preserve original precision and payout/settlement meaning.

Ordinary history/download, permanent bookmarks and saved-scan reopening are deferred. Existing captures, tests and qualification archives remain preserved; their exact replay assertions still apply to those retained tools. Operational configuration and a small durable quota ledger are permitted and necessary. This is not an instruction to delete old data or bypass the current projection's journal assumptions.

Standing owner authority covers necessary new live-data attempts/retries within the sprint plan and quota. Fresh records bind the instruction to each current candidate/scope/budget; spent approvals remain closed. The audit made no new provider attempt. Local fixtures, historic observations, actual live runtime, owner feedback and readiness/release remain separate evidence classes. Production/user readiness and owner acceptance are not claimed until U6 and an explicit owner decision.
