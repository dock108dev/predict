# E2 bounded source qualification — NFL pregame moneyline

**Qualification research complete: September 15, 2026. E2 implementation remains outstanding.** Public official documentation only; no accounts, market requests, collection, services, databases or application changes.

## Decision

**Recommend The Odds API (`the-odds-api.com`) as the first adapter's implementation target, initially restricted to its `pinnacle` bookmaker and one NFL full-game, two-outcome pregame moneyline. Neither path is yet qualified for live fair-price inputs.** This is a supported development recommendation, not a subscription decision or a claim that the source-selection gates for live use have passed.

Exactly two delivery candidates were compared: **Pinnacle's direct API** and **The Odds API multi-book service**. The latter was chosen for comparison because its official NFL guide documents the required sport and paired moneyline [O10]. No alternative provider search is proposed. Using Pinnacle through both paths would still be **one underlying information family**, not two independent opinions [O5; inference].

| Conclusion | Pinnacle direct | The Odds API, initially `pinnacle` only |
|---|---|---|
| Documented suitability | Relevant schema and rules exist, but general public access is closed. Bespoke access, price and durable-use scope are unresolved [P1–P5]. Do not choose this as the first integration dependency. | Suitable for implementing sampled reference capture and durable replay: NFL, bookmaker identity, market read times, history and explicit storage/derived-use permission [O2–O7, O10]. Upstream pair synchronization and complete lineage remain unverified. |
| Account entitlement | Not established. Public documentation is not an issued access agreement. Published ordinary account terms restrict US service; bespoke data-service geographic eligibility is unknown [P1, P4, P7]. | Not established for this owner: no key, plan or account inspected. Published permission supports the proposed personal use without a mandatory support letter [O2]. |
| Live verification | None; no event/market or transport verified. | None; no event ID, actual `pinnacle` availability, ID completeness, refresh behavior or prediction-contract compatibility verified. |

**Why not select a fully usable live source now?** Both lack documented source-side pair atomicity; the aggregator does not establish complete origin/copy lineage or exact applicability of Pinnacle's rules to a returned event. E1 excludes unknown lineage and unassessed terms. A readable paired response alone cannot resolve these facts. The Odds API's public permissions resolve the *documented-use* question, but cannot establish an owner's account entitlement or a successful feed observation.

Scope is one event, not a new multi-book consensus. The [E1 synthetic ATL/PIT example](../evidence/e1/walkthrough.json) remains the offline scenario; its IDs, date and invented rules must not be passed off as a current NFL fixture. Selecting the real game and its native IDs occurs only in a separately authorized live step. Reference prices remain distinct from executable Kalshi/Polymarket US books.

### Settlement assessment for the proposed Pinnacle-origin pair

Pinnacle's general and American Football rules document: overtime included in Game markets; an unoffered draw voids both team bets; a listed draw makes team bets lose on a draw; games not starting within 12 hours of original schedule are void. Suspensions under 55 minutes void Game bets if not completed within 12 hours; after 55 minutes without resumption within 12 hours, the suspension score stands. The text separately voids full-game markets with cumulative quarters below 55 minutes. Venue changes generally void; unplayed forfeits/walkovers void. Market rules outrank sport rules, which outrank general rules [P5].

**Assessment, not provider fact:** retain cancellation/non-start, suspension, relocation, tie and exceptional payouts separately in the existing settlement profile. The exact 55-minute boundary and event-specific exceptions need assessment; do not invent a resolution. A cancellation is not automatically equivalent to a prediction venue's cancellation deadline. Neither `h2h` nor two prices makes refunds equivalent to binary win/loss. Keep rules incomplete and reference ineligible until the actual market and each target's existing profile are compared. No new target settlement research or approval is claimed here.

### Cost and operational bounds

The Odds API currently lists monthly USD plans: **free/500**, **$30/20,000**, **$59/100,000**, **$119/5,000,000**, **$249/15,000,000** credits. Free includes most books but no purchased history; paid tiers list all books and history [O1]. Catalog does not mark `pinnacle` paid-only, but actual free-plan delivery is unverified [O5]. Billing recurs monthly; credits reset on the first of each month [O9]. Terms govern cancellation/refunds [O2].

Current odds cost market count × region-equivalent count; explicit lists of up to ten books count as one region. Historical featured odds cost ten credits per market per region [O3, O7]. Record actual usage headers. A **proposed** three-hour session at one poll/minute, one book and `h2h`, would budget 180 odds calls/credits, plus bounded retries; start/end polling conventions can add a call. Thirty-six historical snapshots would cost 360 credits. These are planning calculations, not measured usage or a purchase recommendation. Free quota can support a small capture if the selected book is available; $30 is the smallest listed history-enabled plan. No need to purchase history to build local history prospectively.

Quota exhaustion has a documented `OUT_OF_USAGE_CREDITS` error; no automatic metered-overage price was found [O8]. Treat remaining budget as a hard collection bound; do not assume paid overage or authorize automatic upgrades. Pinnacle bespoke cost/quotas/history/overage are all unknown. The two-minute direct API limit and aggregator minute-scale refresh cannot support subsecond lead/lag claims. The deferred 250 ms project gate remains deferred.

### Immutable records and recovery

Implement the tables/records proposed in [E2 handoff](e2-handoff.md), with these concrete additions:

- **`reference_source_revision`:** provider/book key, origin/family/copies, identity kind, exact evidence URLs/hashes, effective time and **known-at** time. Store assessed and unknown lineage distinctly. A later source/rule fact produces a new revision; it cannot rewrite an old estimate. Known same-book paths deduplicate. Catalog prediction keys must be excluded from the reference allowlist; `polymarket` must not be silently equated with Polymarket US.
- **`reference_receipt`:** session, ingress ID/time, exact raw artifact, sanitized request scope, response status, usage headers, source time/semantics, mode and entitlement binding. Retain every response, including repeats and non-opportunities. Shared identical body hashes must not collapse arrival records. Persist rejected responses too.
- **`reference_quote_revision`:** receipt + enrichment ID, normalizer/odds-conversion version, participant mapping evidence, native locator/ID kinds, ordered prices, side-time nulls, pairing status, rule hash, source-registry revision, unknowns/rejection reasons and enrichment **known-at**. `ReferenceQuote` is the immutable view of that binding. E1's quote eligibility does not itself enforce the new side-pair policy; E2/E3 must not silently mark unverified pairing eligible.
- **`reference_gap`:** assign local gap/session ID; provider/event/market/family scope; detected time; nullable start/end; reason; recovery state; raw receipt/evidence links. Differentiate transport timeout, 429, exhausted quota, empty book/market, one-sided/malformed payload, disk/queue failure and unknown history span. Ordinary polling intervals are sampled coverage, not proof of feed failure. Silence never establishes suspension, cancellation or exact outage onset.
- **Recovery:** mark the first observed failure time, retain last success as an observation bound, leave actual outage start unknown. Retry transient errors with finite backoff/budget and honor any response retry guidance. Stop on entitlement/quota failures. Resume with a fresh complete snapshot and record its receipt as observed recovery, not as repair of the unseen interval. A one-sided changed snapshot may preserve a null-side quote but cannot carry the old counterpart. Stop remains cancellable during requests/backoff.
- **History/revisions:** archive snapshot time and previous/next available pointers independently from market read time and actual download receipt time. Re-fetching the same historical snapshot with different bytes creates a new receipt/revision. Holes and corrections remain visible. No interpolation or filled gaps masquerading as observations. Guaranteed source retention is unknown even though indefinite *local* retention is permitted.
- **Replay/export:** retain receipt, quote-enrichment, source, matching, rule and gap IDs with hashes. Include revisions known at the estimate, not today's mappings. Enforce `received_at <= as_of` and enrichment/source knowledge cutoffs. Preserve existing `capture-bundle-1` readers; use an explicit new version. Reserve `fair_price`/input linkage for E3. Local replay bundles are distinct from distributing a raw data product.

**Historical-time example (design):** a snapshot dated September 1 downloaded September 15 has September 15 `received_at`. It cannot enter a September 1 receipt-cutoff estimate. A retrospective source-time study requires an explicitly separate historical methodology with unavailable local receipt latency; never backdate receipt times to make E1 accept it. Synthetic event clocks remain labeled synthetic.
