# Prices and executable liquidity

Predict shows observed prices and modeled comparisons. The ordinary opportunity
board does not establish how much can be acquired at a displayed price. An
attractive quote, repeated venue appearance or historical trading volume is not
proof of available size.

## What the board establishes

Stored rows retain the quoted price, source and receipt clocks, exact selection,
fee evidence and any qualified probability reference. EV describes those modeled
inputs. Arbitrage compares supported opposing cashflows after fees, using a bounded
illustrative allocation. Neither calculation confirms that both legs can be filled.

Browse and exact comparisons can show prices without a complete fair reference;
EV and fair odds remain unavailable. A missing reference is not zero EV. Predict
records manually reported fills in My Bets but does not submit orders or verify
execution through an account connection.

## Interpreting depth and amounts

Native order-book observations have their own event/outcome identity, quantity
units, payout model, source time, receipt time and sequence. They must not be
attached to an aggregate quote without an exact, qualified binding. Different
venues can use different contract payout units; price percentages alone do not
establish equivalent cash amounts.

Comparisons put **Available size: Unknown** beside the amount when the observation
has no price-specific quantity. Aggregate American odds and historical volume do
not supply that evidence. For directly sourced Kalshi/Polymarket US quotes, a linked
current, active, synchronized native top ask at the identical price can show its
observed contracts and independent book source/receipt timestamps. This is observed
book evidence, not a fill promise. A different price, missing binding or unavailable
book keeps the Unknown state.

A top-level quantity supports only that captured level. Larger amounts may cross
levels, pay different fees, fail a legal quantity grid or leave cash unspent.
Displayed depth can change before an order is accepted. Account limits and actual
fills remain separate from an observed book.

For two-leg comparisons, both legal quantities and payout terms matter. A partial
fill leaves directional exposure; independently observed books need not have been
simultaneously available. Push, void, tie and cancellation treatment can differ
from decisive completed-game assumptions. The current board does not certify
amount-adjusted execution, synchronized fills or a realizable positive edge.
