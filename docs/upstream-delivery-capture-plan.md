# Bounded upstream-delivery evidence capture — proposal

September 19, 2026. **Offline plan only; not authorization and not executable with current tooling. Next action: engineering.**

## Question and scope

For one supported Kalshi NFL pregame full-game winner market, when the unchanged 30-second receipt-freshness rule expires, is its last primary-stream book consistent with independently requested native state, is a specific change absent from the captured primary stream, or did a received update fail locally between receive and admission?

The [retained diagnosis](../evidence/coverage-diagnosis-20260919/diagnosis.md) reconciles all 44 historical pre-Stop losses as receipt-freshness expiry (41 Kalshi, 3 US). It does not identify why qualifying updates stopped. The [Kalshi repair](../evidence/kalshi-expiry-repair-20260919/report.md) fixes expiry scheduling under sibling traffic, not upstream delivery. Neither historical result will be rewritten by this experiment.

Propose **one Kalshi market, one primary WebSocket, one independent REST book sampler**, initial discovery plus one safety refresh at Start+120 seconds. Direct Stop at Start+240 seconds; independent intake cutoff at Start+300 seconds, including discovery. No second venue, paid feed, secondary WebSocket, cross-venue matching requirement, trading, ranking integration or automatic replacement attempt. A single market can expose a discrepancy or local loss; it cannot reproduce every multi-market delivery condition. No stale episode or no reference change is a valid inconclusive result.

## Deterministic selection and schedule

1. Use the existing bounded KXNFLGAME catalog traversal and native pregame validation. Exclude parse/identity ambiguities, unsupported/non-full-game markets, non-active status, unknown kickoff, catalog exclusions and absent supported purchase sides. Require kickoff **later than Start UTC +600 seconds**, preserving at least the existing 300-second margin through the maximum 300-second intake. Expiration is not kickoff.
2. Sort qualifying markets by normalized scheduled kickoff UTC, then exact native ticker, following `app/collection/continuous.py:select_inventory`; select the first one. Do not choose after observing price activity or staleness. Record all exclusions and the traversed universe; a truncated catalog is not a census. Require the bounded traversal needed for selection to complete; no silent fallback to partial discovery. Historical expired tickers are not pinned as current candidates.
3. Freeze that ticker for the attempt. Maximum selected markets =1, distinct stream groups =1, simultaneous WS sockets =1, WS connection attempts =1. No reconnect/replacement/resubscribe after a transport or sequence failure: stop and preserve evidence. Initial subscription/snapshot is required by Start+60 seconds; otherwise stop as insufficient startup evidence. Zero eligible markets, catalog cap, unavailable schedule or incomplete discovery also stops; do not broaden sport, venue or eligibility. One eligible market is sufficient.
4. Reserve at most **40 initial REST attempts**, including the two existing account-limit/endpoint-cost lookups, catalog pages/details and all retries. At Start+120 seconds perform the existing safety/catalog refresh, at most **40 further attempts**, without replacing or expanding the selection. Revalidate the selected market; changed schedule/status or uncertain continued eligibility stops the experiment. References never refresh eligibility.
5. Reference slots are Start+60, +75, …, +285 seconds: **16 maximum**, normally **12** before the direct Stop at +240. Use one request per slot, no reference retries, no catch-up bursts, no overlapping HTTP requests. A slot blocked by discovery/pacing/in-flight work is explicitly skipped, not shifted into a burst. HTTP timeout at most 5 seconds and bounded by remaining intake time. Discovery and references share one serial pacing/account-budget arbiter; at most 2 requests/second and slower when the established account-cost rule requires it. No sampler traffic before Start or after Stop.
6. A failed reference (timeout, HTTP failure, malformed/incomparable image) is retained and ends intake; a response taking more than 2 seconds is labelled timing-uncertain and excluded from strong temporal attribution. Skipped/delayed slots and cache uncertainty can make the run inconclusive. 429/access/account-cost failure stops with no escalation. No traffic merely to discover an entitlement outside these counted lookups.

## Correlated records and clocks

Add bounded instrumentation at the transport boundary **before parsing and admission**, not only at `producer.emit`. The current `prediction_frame` journal record precedes parsing but is itself subject to admission and cannot prove that every received body was retained. New instrumentation must fail closed on its own retention failure and identify the resulting blind interval.

For every delivered native application frame retain original bytes once, SHA-256, size, connection/group/local receive ID, ticker when parseable, opcode/type, UTC and monotonic receive stamps, transport exception/close context, and links to all downstream records. Preserve malformed and irrelevant frames with disposition; redact secrets at the established boundary, marking suppression as incomplete evidence. Application `recv` return is not a packet-arrival timestamp. Library buffering, scheduling and backpressure remain possible before that boundary. Protocol ping/pong instrumentation is proposed only where the library exposes it reliably; record unsupported visibility explicitly, never synthesize health evidence.

Retain outgoing subscription command IDs and requested tickers; native acknowledgements/errors, `sid`/`seq` when present, snapshot/delta identities, recovery/gap decisions, parser begin/end and result, normalized book ID, each admission decision/reason, journal acknowledgement, queue insertion/drain and coverage mutation clocks. Distinguish expected control/non-qualifying messages from lost qualifying updates. Save native side/price/quantity and exact reconstruction inputs, including zero deltas and unchanged images. Record native source timestamps as supplied, with unknown semantics where applicable; no invented per-market sequence guarantee from subscription-scoped Kalshi sequences.

Record connect attempt/open, subscribe/ack, initial synchronization, liveness observations, errors, stream closure, event-loop lag and queue/backpressure measurements, catalog generations, eligibility changes, expiry and direct Stop/cutoff. Existing post-book `connected` rows are not independent health proof. Finite diagnostic rings are summaries; authoritative correlation records belong to bounded durable history.

Each reference gets an ID, scheduled slot, request-send and response-first/last-byte UTC/monotonic times, completion/timeout, status, safe cache/Date headers if supplied, byte count/hash and native body. Record its parsed canonical comparison window and comparison algorithm version. Retain both last primary state before send and primary states through response completion; later primary observations remain separate. A request interval overlapping a primary change has ambiguous ordering. Do not manufacture latency from HTTP Date, metadata time or a last-change timestamp.

Capture paired UTC/monotonic anchors at actual Start (not a later status request), each reference, refresh and Stop. Bracket UTC reads with monotonic samples; retain bracket width, clock resolution and observed offset drift. Use monotonic ordering and elapsed deadlines within the process; a helper uses its own paired anchors and reports alignment uncertainty. UTC jumps or ambiguous cross-process order invalidate strong timing conclusions. No network time query. For each comparison report request duration, alignment uncertainty, missing records and unbounded server-state age; local timing precision cannot eliminate the last limitation.

Reference records have a separate diagnostic type/namespace. They must never enter the primary adapter's last-receipt clock, coverage usability, production snapshots, opportunities or rankings. Primary freshness remains strict age >30 seconds with the repaired timer. Do not make a stale primary book recent by polling REST.

## Start, Stop and attempt lifecycle

Existing building blocks: `supervised_live.Control`, `CoverageOwner`, `ContinuousSession`, paced `REST`, `PredictionBudget`, segmented journal/replay and `finalization.write_supplement`. **Existing live validation requires both venues, duration300/refresh120 and reference disabled. It is not a command for this proposal.** Implement a narrowly isolated diagnostic entry point/profile selector reusing those controls; preserve current defaults, old live validators, mock credential guards and old attempts. The existing reference flag for other feeds is not permission to activate this sampler.

Before any future Start: freeze exact candidate/spec/endpoint allowlist, preflight resources, confirm idle exclusive owner and direct Stop availability; reject conflicts. Preserve the stopped beta. Only after separate owner authorization may the established Kalshi project credential reference be loaded; no US credential access. Persist a new exclusive one-shot attempt marker before any venue traffic; consume it on failed Start, failed discovery, no eligible market, early Stop or crash. No marker deletion/reset, reuse, auto-resume or new output directory as a retry workaround. A later attempt requires another explicit authorization.

Start itself sets the monotonic origin and starts the independent cutoff supervisor. Direct Stop is available throughout discovery, HTTP waits, raw retention and finalization. At240 seconds issue direct Stop through the control path; record intended, sent, received and intake-closed clocks. It must not depend on a stale event or reference callback. Independent supervisor enforces300 seconds even if the collector loop stalls; offline prove cancellation/escalation behavior. If direct Stop fails, report that failure even if cutoff works. Never treat an internal duration exit as successful direct Stop.

Stop on any resource reservation/rate/cost failure, unretained receive or admission failure, disk/memory threshold, sequence gap, connection closure, missing ack/snapshot by60, failed reference, invalid identity/schema/eligibility, failed refresh, journal/manifest failure, explicit owner Stop or deadline. A late reference is retained as uncertain; it cannot extend the session. Subscription errors or requested/acknowledged mismatch stop. Do not reconnect or replace the market to recover the experiment.

Cancel and await the one pending native receive, outstanding HTTP operation and scheduler; close and join sockets/tasks; stop issuing requests immediately; drain already admitted work. Target intake close<=1 second and all task/feed close/drain<=5 seconds. On failure preserve the unresolved suffix, not a fabricated clean terminal. Release ownership and remove only the new ephemeral control socket after closure; keep evidence and attempt marker. Supervisor escalation may leave an incomplete journal and must never claim clean drain.

Finalization is offline: verify segment hashes and exact identities, replay native inputs incrementally, reconcile primary observations and diagnostic comparisons, then write bounded report/receipt. Require received = accepted + rejected and accepted = durable = drained for successful completion, zero pending/unresolved/durable-not-queued, plus separate raw-receive-to-parse-to-observation disposition reconciliation (a control frame need not emit a book). Classify reference attempts = success + failure + cancelled; scheduled slots = attempted + skipped + not reached. Reconcile byte reservations/releases, all retained output and cumulative writes, including replaced manifests, supplemental serialization and the fixed16 KiB receipt. Preserve `write_supplement`'s explicit exclusion of receipt creation from its own memory/timing boundary; do not claim lifetime peak from it. Independent supervisor records outer completion timing without redefining that receipt. If the300-second finalization cutoff or any finalization cap fails, retain incomplete results and consume the attempt.
