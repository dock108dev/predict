# SC08 / Q04 — read-only data and comparison review

October 17, 2026. Review the ordinary app against the current
[read-only requirements](practical-use-requirements.md). The owner's actual dated
product decision remains open; historical tests do not supply it.

1. Open [Predict](http://127.0.0.1:8783/) and inspect available supported games,
   source-native positions, observed prices, source ages and source health.
2. Use venue/game/market filters and open Details. Confirm that authentic prices
   remain visible when estimates have missing inputs, with those inputs labeled.
3. Inspect available observed depth and price comparisons. Check that modeled
   fees, amounts, EV and conditional/covered economics identify their assumptions
   and applicability; unknown economics do not become guaranteed profit.
4. Close/reopen the app while the data runtime continues. Observe available latest
   data and original clocks. An ordinary natural change may update the screen;
   retained data stays last-known when stale or collection is explicitly stopped.
5. Check affected desktop/390px presentation, focus and source status. Optional
   source links should identify their actual destination scope.
6. Give the actual date/version and decision: accepted, accepted with limitations,
   or changes requested with one reproducible example.

Usefulness of collected data and comparisons is the review target. Venue order
entry, personal-account specifications and provider replies are outside the
current product scope. Aggregate accounting and hosted CI retain separate status.
