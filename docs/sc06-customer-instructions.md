# Watching opportunities in Predict

Choose supported venues and market filters, then watch the global ranking. Both execution positions must use included venues. **Find on this page** searches only the displayed page; it does not narrow historical observations or arrival cues.

Open **Scope, size & observations** to see retained distinct episodes in your selected category and filters. “Newly qualified” requires an observed prior check without that category. “Present at first observation” has unknown earlier history. A coverage gap, expiry, Stop or restart leaves history incomplete. A checked current zero is different from unknown coverage and from the number observed earlier.

Edge and supported funding ranges describe observed allocations. Their largest values may occur at different times. If both minimum filters cannot be established from retained joint observations, the summary says historical threshold matches are incomplete. Observed spans run from first to last qualifying observations; they do not establish an uninterrupted market lifetime or a reliable frequency.

Select a current row to inspect its manual ticket. Review native actions, funding, category and loss conditions. Changing Amount checks a separate replacement; adopt it explicitly after review. Copying allocations requires the current ticket's authority and qualified destinations. Original clocks, expiry and Stop still control readiness. Nothing reserves liquidity or submits an order.

Sound starts off and can be enabled deliberately. Reloads, filtering, reconnects and lost continuity start silently. Summary reads and filter changes do not request source data. Observations retain up to seven days, 10,000 episodes or 10 MiB, whichever limit is reached first; older eviction and gaps are disclosed. Actual current source/model/destination qualification and SC08/Q04 owner review remain separate gates.
