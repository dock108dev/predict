## 3. Calculation contract

**Baseline:** request decimal odds. For two finite odds greater than one, `uA=1/dA`, `uB=1/dB`, `S=uA+uB`, overround `S−1`, and `pA=uA/S=dB/(dA+dB)`, `pB=1−pA`. Treat these as estimates conditional on `N`: an ordinary completed team-winner result under the assessed rules. Equal proportional margin removal is a model assumption, not calibration. One family has weight one; disagreement/confidence statistics are unavailable.

Retain exact raw Decimal spellings. Reuse E3's 50-significant-digit half-even arithmetic and 18-decimal-place probability outputs, checking sum equals one. E4 cashflow arithmetic uses 100 significant digits, half-even; exact time arithmetic preserves native precision. Probability/overround are fractions; prices are USD per $1 payout contract; quantity is contracts. Round monetary charges only at the fee engine's required grouping/precision, never through a display value. Display USD to cents and percent to two places; stored ROI is a fraction. Keep mathematical sign before display rounding.

**Public-page research freshness policy (`public-page-delayed-research-1`):** this source path supersedes the earlier 90-second receipt / 120-second provider-read proposal. Receipt age at cutoff is at most **1,200 seconds (20 minutes)**. If an explicitly scoped bookmaker update timestamp is displayed, its age must be at most **1,200 seconds**, and it cannot be after retrieval. A documented or clearly disclosed approximate delay up to **900 seconds (15 minutes)** is acceptable when the bookmaker timestamp is absent; leave that timestamp and actual upstream age unknown. Receipt age and disclosed delay are separate facts, not a verified combined age bound. If both update time and disclosed delay are absent, preserve the pair as evidence but mark temporal eligibility unavailable. Article update text and HTTP retrieval time never substitute for bookmaker update time. The captured source currently has this limitation.

Target receipt age remains at most **30 seconds** and absolute reference/target receipt skew at most **1,200 seconds**. These parameters permit research comparison, not contemporaneous execution claims. Label all resulting probabilities and EV **delayed-reference research estimates**; comparing delayed odds to a newer target can create apparent discrepancies. Never claim a verified current executable edge. The implemented source time assessor does not replace the remaining prediction-record gates.

Select the latest receipt before eligibility by exact time/ID, then validate it; no older-good fallback or cross-capture side joins. Retain all considered/excluded evidence. All receipt, assessment, rule and fee knowledge must be at/before cutoff. Estimate and actual save must occur before kickoff minus 60 seconds; stop on uncertain phase/reschedule. Unknown target-independent lineage, ordinary-winner rules, fees, exceptional settlement or prospective evidence retain their existing unavailable behavior. One bookmaker family remains one family across delivery paths. The public parser does not promote co-display into verified upstream synchronization or feed the synthetic-only E2/E3 path.

**Purchase:** initial target is one explicitly identified Kalshi YES team contract, with an explicit positive whole quantity (proposed pilot: 10). Walk actual asks; Kalshi asks are `1−opposite bid` at unchanged quantity. Record all consumed levels. Insufficient depth makes the requested calculation unavailable; show capacity without silently reducing size. No midpoint, last trade, sportsbook limit or unsupported Short becomes purchase liquidity. No extra impact charge after depth walking; assumed fills remain hypothetical.

Let `C=Σ(q_i × ask_i)`, `F_entry` be applicable entry charges and `D` be the fee engine's single-target entry cash requirement, including any temporary cash needed before rounding refunds. For each outcome `s`, retain net cashflow `G_s` including entry costs, payout, settlement charges and supported fee credits/refunds. Conditional EV is `pA×G_A + (1−pA)×G_B` after exact target orientation; return is `EV_N/D`, per-contract net is `EV_N/q`. For ordinary $1/$0 payout with no later charges or refunds and `D=C+F_entry`, this simplifies to `q×p_target−D`; break-even is `D/q`. Otherwise solve from the two state cashflows. No double subtraction of entry cost.

Fees require exact venue/product/taker schedule, effective time, market/event override assessment, account rounding basis and one declared hypothetical order/fill partition. Unknown account applicability may support a clearly named fee scenario, not a verified net result. Unknown material charge leaves that scenario's net/ROI null. No default zero fee, settlement levy, rebate or current reuse of the historical pinned schedule.

**Exceptional outcomes:** keep tie, void/refund, cancellation, postponement, abandonment and discretionary resolution distinct. A refund is cost-dependent and may not refund fees. A $0.50 tie payout is not a stake refund. If exceptional probabilities `m_j` and cashflows are supported, exhaustive mutually exclusive states give `EV=(1−Σm_j)EV_N+Σm_jG_j`. Without them, unconditional EV and contract fair value stay null. Do not sum overlapping exceptional labels; existing scenario names alone do not establish a probability partition. Unknown material payouts/costs are not erased by assigning zero mass.

### Independently worked illustrative example — not market evidence

Invented odds: A `1.80=9/5`, B `2.10=21/10`. Thus `uA=5/9`, `uB=10/21`, sum `65/63`, overround `2/63≈3.174603%`. Independently, `pA=2.10/3.90=7/13=0.538461538461538462`, `pB=6/13`. This rational derivation is independent of application outputs.

Invented target asks: 6 contracts at $0.54 and 4 at $0.55; requested 10 consumes $3.24+$2.20=$5.44. Assume, solely for this example, a verified total entry charge of $0.16, no later charges/refunds and ordinary $1/$0 payout. This is **not a claim about a Kalshi fee schedule**. `D=$5.60`, `G_A=$4.40`, `G_B=−$5.60`. Exact unrounded-probability EV is `(7/13)×4.40+(6/13)×(−5.60)=−14/65≈−$0.215384615384615385`; ROI `−1/26≈−3.846154%`; per-contract net `−$0.02153846`; break-even 56%. Stored 18-place p produces `−0.215384615384615380` at 100-digit arithmetic; display is **−$0.22, −3.85%**.

Material missing-input variants: remove the $0.16 fee evidence and retain only $5.44 cost and approximately $5.384615 conditional gross payout; net/ROI/break-even are null. Even with the assumed fee, unknown tie/void mass means unconditional EV is null. If tie mass is `t`, all other exceptions absent *by a separately labeled hypothetical assumption*, and tie gross payout is $5 with fees retained, then `EV=(1−t)(−14/65)+t(−0.60)`; do not choose `t=0` by default. Original observations are never altered to improve EV.

## 4. Save first, evaluate later

Only three logical records, with existing dependency bundles embedded or linked:

1. **Prediction:** immutable ID/hash, mode, canonical/native event and outcome IDs, kickoff revision, target venue/market/YES predicate and $1 unit; reference/target receipt IDs, source/receipt times, raw hashes, paired odds/ask ladders; source family/copy, matching/rules and knowledge revisions; cutoff, estimated-at and actual saved-at; method/policy version, parameters, included/excluded inputs; conditional probability/basis; requested/usable quantity, fills, fee context, cashflows, denominator, result/status/reasons. Include the preregistered evaluation cohort/selection rule and baseline probability. No secrets. References arriving later cannot enter this record.
2. **Sporting outcome annotation:** prediction/event link; final score, team winner/tie/cancelled/unresolved; official result source URL/artifact, published/observed/recorded times, status and revision. Manual entry from an official NFL game result is sufficient later; no result API integration now.
3. **Contract settlement annotation:** target contract link; actual venue resolution/payout per contract, refund/discretionary basis, evidence/time and finality. Keep separate from sporting outcome; do not infer settlement from the winner. There is no actual fill or realized trading profit in this read-only pilot.

Append corrections with `supersedes_id`, reason and recorded-at; never update old predictions or backdate knowledge. Later settlement corrections create new evaluation versions. Hashing supports replay/integrity, not external proof of creation time. Persist successfully before showing a prediction as saved; a write failure is not a retained forecast. Historical captures/reanalysis are labeled retrospective and excluded from prospective scores.

**Proposed pilot:** predeclare up to ten NFL games in chronological kickoff order within one chosen week that have the named reference and a matched Kalshi YES target; one designated target per game fixed before looking at EV. One primary forecast at the first attempted capture in the 30-to-20-minute pregame window; if unavailable, retain that result without replacing it with a more favorable later attempt. Additional observations are exploratory and not additional independent games. Stop at ten games or week's end; report missing/failed attempts and selection coverage. A one-event fixture proves plumbing; ten real games would still not establish a profitable strategy.

**Score:** for games satisfying the predeclared ordinary-result conditioning set, binary Brier score `(pA−yA)^2`, lower is better. Store baseline `pA=0.5` in advance; compare mean model Brier with baseline mean 0.25 on exactly the same games and report paired differences/counts. Score the sporting event once, not both complementary teams, contracts or captures. Ties/voids and unresolved games are separately counted with reasons, not coded losses or quietly omitted. Eligibility for this conditional sporting score and for contract settlement evaluation remains distinct.

Report probability score separately from any coarse calibration summary; tiny samples cannot support a calibration claim. Conditional modeled EV is not a probability score. Optional later closing-price comparisons require separately timestamped comparable closing observations; missing closes stay missing, with no paid history/backfill and no use in earlier estimates. Paper settlement cashflow under assumed fills/fees is not realized trading profit. Do not add closing collection or a profitability threshold to completion.
