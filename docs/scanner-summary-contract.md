# Observed opportunity summaries v1

`GET /api/scanner/summaries` returns one bounded aggregate for the selected scanner filters. [Schema](contracts/predict-opportunity-summaries-1.schema.json), [authored count examples](examples/opportunity-summaries-1/README.md), [customer guidance](scanner-usage.md). This read performs no provider request or financial calculation, does not start the scanner, and does not renew a quote, ticket or source clock.

The query accepts only `venues`, `sports`, `leagues`, `markets`, `periods`, `category`, `minimum_edge` and `minimum_size`, with the scanner's existing validation and both-leg venue rule. Pagination and Find do not change the summary scope. Categories remain separate: a conditional hedge or EV episode never increases the covered-arb count. Scope identity binds canonical filters. `server_id`, `epoch` and `observed_at` identify the latest observed publication and are null before the first observation. The client rejects another nonnull server/epoch or crossed filter response.

The response holds counts, exact rational edge/funding extrema, the range of first-to-last observed qualification spans, the count of episodes with observed onset and authoritative withdrawal, observation window, current selected-scope pair coverage and explicit history loss. `state=unavailable` can accompany useful retained observations: it withholds a claim of complete current coverage. A null `coverage.checked_zero` means unknown. `true` means this complete current selected scope had no currently matching rows; historical retained episodes may still be positive. It does not mean no opportunity existed during gaps. Zero compatible pairs cannot establish checked-zero.

Coverage is selected-pair and category specific. Completed selected pairs can establish their scope while unrelated pairs remain pending, subject to selected scope readiness, original expiry and global failures that invalidate the publication. A cashflow evaluation receipt never establishes EV-reference coverage or an EV checked-zero. EV coverage requires a supported current numeric EV row for each selected pair; missing or last-known EV is unknown coverage. EV is signed research, so a negative expectation remains an EV research row unless the selected minimum-edge filter excludes it. Losing the current EV reference ends the EV episode with a gap rather than an authoritative economic withdrawal; reappearance begins left-censored.

`observation_window.from/to` are the store's first/latest publication timestamps. They describe when observation started and last advanced; they are not a denominator proving continuous selected-scope monitoring. `coverage.observed_complete_publications` is a global complete-publication counter, not a filtered pair-time denominator. No frequency, average episode duration, fill, realized gain, unseen-opportunity estimate or guaranteed return is supplied.

## Episode ownership and meaning

The background projection directly offers a detached, allowlisted publication observation to one bounded worker. The app reads a replaceable PostgreSQL mirror of compact summary records and metadata; it never creates another file owner. [Cross-process contract](data-app-runtime-contract.md). Persistence runs outside projection and ingress locks. Neither ranked-page comparisons nor transition HTTP responses create episodes. The bounded ticket client transport journal travels in the latest SQL publication and remains independent of this episode store.

An episode binds exact scanner opportunity/category row and compatible pair, server/epoch, original clocks and dependency/economic revisions. Initial presence, newly encountered pairs and the first observations after lost continuity are left-censored. An observed complete prior evaluation of the same pair without that category is required for `observed_new`. Valid current category rows are observed even while other pairs remain pending or unavailable. Pending financial reconciliation of that same pair preserves its prior presence or complete negative proof and cannot establish withdrawal. An authoritative complete same-pair evaluation without the category ends it as withdrawn; an absent row with nonpending incomplete or missing pair coverage ends observation with an unknown gap. Unrelated pair failures cannot erase a valid observed row. Stop, capture failure, restart and epoch changes censor the affected continuity. Threshold crossings, filters, reloads, pagination, receipt churn and alert cooldowns do not create episodes or suppress extrema updates.

The compact record updates in place: first/latest qualification, original/latest revisions and clocks, edge/funding minima and maxima, meaningful revision-change count, termination and censoring. First-to-last observed spans are lower bounds on the observed qualifying episode's possible duration; their minimum/maximum are a range across records, never a guaranteed uninterrupted market lifetime. Left- or right-censored episodes are not averaged as complete lifetimes.

Independent maximum edge and maximum supported funding can occur at different observations. A bounded Pareto frontier of at most four jointly witnessed `(edge,size)` values supports historical conjunction queries. A record is counted for simultaneous minimum-edge/minimum-size filters only when one retained observation witnessed both. When discarded evidence could affect the answer, `history.threshold_history_complete=false`; the observed count is incomplete, not a confident zero. A witness dominated by another retained witness is safely redundant.

## Persistence, retention and failure

The summary owner is `app/comparison/scanner_summaries.py`; its explicitly summary-owned local file is `.local/scanner-summary/episodes.sc06`. It contains a 4 KiB checksummed metadata block and 4 KiB checksummed episode slots, with no separate index, journal, WAL, archive or auxiliary file. Summary schema v1 is additive local storage; it does not change PostgreSQL migration history or business tables. Physical byte accounting uses the greater of file size and allocated filesystem blocks, retaining the observed high water.

The first reached limit wins: seven days from episode `first_observed`, 10,000 episodes, or 10 MiB including metadata and slots. The default reserves 2 MiB inside that physical limit for filesystem allocation behavior, leaving an 8 MiB logical file with 2,047 episode slots. Actual allocated blocks are checked as well as file length; unusual physical growth fails the optional summary owner independently of current prices. Reduced disposable caps below 1 MiB use no filesystem reserve and remain subject to their actual physical guard. Eviction orders by `first_observed`, then stable episode ID, and discloses lost history and cumulative eviction. Retention equality expires the record. Read selection excludes expired records; cleanup belongs to the worker. Cleared slots are reused rather than appended, so unchanged updates and repeated reads do not create a growing history. The file can remain at its bounded physical high water after eviction.

One worker receives at most four detached observations of at most 4 MiB each. Overflow discards continuity and censors the retained stream. Active episode state is bounded by stored slots, and live rows/pairs by the scanner's 4,096/2,048 caps. Responses are at most 64 KiB. One HTTP summary read may run at a time; cancelled requests do not release its capacity while detached work remains active. Counts are safe integers and error notes are bounded codes, never raw provider data, credentials, ladders or expanded reports.

A checksum failure, failed write, overflow or unavailable owner withholds complete coverage/history. Restart censors formerly open records; it cannot resume an inferred uninterrupted episode. Summary failure must not stop ordinary ingress, financial discovery, expiry, Stop or manual tickets. Summary writes never acquire a provider or revise economics.

## Customer surface and browser observations

The existing **Scope, size & observations** disclosure shows retained observed episode counts, edge/funding ranges, observed spans, window and coverage/censoring. Collapsed disclosure adds no result-row height. It makes at most one optional read every two seconds while open and visible, with a 1.8-second deadline and 64 KiB streaming-body limit. Scope changes, Stop, suspension and supersession abort or invalidate old responses. Optional failure cannot delay scanner revision application. The scanner, manual ticket, explicit adoption, native copying and sound controls retain their own authority.

`predict:scanner-paint` revision events include exact delivered row/event/pair/native identities, dependency/economic revisions, original clocks, calculation clocks and projection/SQL causal evidence. They record body availability, acceptance, DOM application and a second-animation-frame observation on the browser's monotonic clock, plus the wall-clock value at that observation. This is a browser rendering opportunity, not physical display scanout. The diagnostic accessor retains at most 256 samples/2 MiB and discloses emitted, dropped-sample and dropped-byte counters. The byte cap can retain fewer than 256 large pages. Every sample is still emitted as an event; performance harnesses retain their individually matched samples independently and cannot infer a complete campaign from an evicted accessor history. Python and browser monotonic clocks require measured clock correlation and uncertainty; they are never directly subtracted.
