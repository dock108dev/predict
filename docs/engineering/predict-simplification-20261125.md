# Predict simplification

Dashboard, Data and Experiments are the three destinations. Collection controls
are available from the collection status in the header. The installed workspace
serves the three page module graphs and the collection page. Its route table
separates stored evidence readers from collection controls.

## Inventory and disposition

| Area | Disposition | Evidence or expected use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard, EV, Opportunities and Compare | Dashboard is home. Old EV and Opportunities URLs lead to Dashboard. The old compare desk, amount/preview editors, live update transport and current-comparison lease/settings routes leave the installed app. | The board reads the committed current offers and keeps the exact calculation and original-pull identity visible. |
| Data, Browse Odds, Raw Odds, Game, Replay, retained and Admin database | Data holds quotes, original acquisitions, results, frozen captures and bounded PostgreSQL inspection. URLs with evidence selections carry those selections forward. Raw native evidence and exact saved-capture reads are retained. | Historical IDs, source observations and result receipts are necessary to explain and reproduce experiment selections. Data and exports use stored evidence. |
| Research home, suggestions, old experiment tabs and independent Kelly campaigns | Experiments opens Win $10, with Kelly as its paired sizing. New UI-created campaigns can appear there. Superseded campaigns leave default discovery and replay scheduling. Exact historical IDs still open their saved runs and results. | The canonical campaign and new experiments need immutable configurations and saved grades. Older configurations and reports remain research evidence. |
| Arbitrage board, Scanner, manual tickets, Watchlists, My Bets, About and Trading Fees | Standalone pages and operational endpoints leave the installed workspace. Existing arb calculations remain available as stored data. Scanner, journal quote sampling and the standalone comparison coverage publisher do not start in the installed collector. | The workflows have no caller for these standalone pages or jobs. Offline model and qualification harnesses still use the retained calculation implementations and evidence formats. Historical client fixture files are excluded from the installed route allowlist. |
| Activity-based Live control and manual aggregate refresh | Remove activity renewal, mode switching and manual aggregate refresh from the installed routes. Display the stored schedule and discovery interval. | Opening or reloading a workspace cannot renew an activity lease or start a pull. Scheduled discovery and checkpoint workers remain necessary. |
| Stop, source pauses, account recovery and preferences | Keep existing controls behind the secondary collection page. | These are persistent owner and accounting decisions. The app delegates mutations to the existing guarded control path. |
| Ordinary calculation owner, first-observation and T−10 scheduler, source results, fee metadata, paper replay and grader, archive | Keep the existing collection and publication owners. | Current source evidence, reference and fee explanations, saved comparisons, the Data coverage census, paper replay/grades and archive retention have active callers. |

## Configuration retained

No new presentation feature flags are needed. The retired UI and jobs are
removed from the installed startup and routing paths.

| Existing setting | Why it remains | Removal condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collector `enabled`, aggregate enablement and source routing, Stop and source pauses | They govern which source can dispatch, including recovery of the saved account state. The installed owner uses the aggregate route and keeps direct native source switches off. | Keep while collecting or carrying unresolved exposure. A source route can be removed after its supported collection responsibilities and recovery records have a successor. |
| `live_enabled` | Defaults off. Legacy native qualification and source state handling still read the phase policy. The normal workspace uses the pregame schedule and exposes no Live switch. | Remove when the collector and retained-data readers no longer consume phase-specific authority or the supported alternate source qualification is removed. |
| Fee switch | The owner can compare calculations with recorded fee assumptions. The setting schedules only local revaluation; historical quotes and campaign assumptions retain their evidence. | Remove if one fee policy becomes the only supported collection and paper assumption and the legacy interpretations remain reproducible. |
| Archive capture and profile | New retained evidence must continue to reach the independently stored archive under the installed configuration. | Remove only if a replacement takes ownership of archived evidence and verifies completeness at cutover. |

## Verification boundaries

Browse and export checks must reproduce exact stored records and report zero
provider requests. Collection verification observes the first ordinary accepted
receipt or checkpoint after restart and checks saved controls and accounting. A
new experiment is created and run only with disposable state.
