## Implemented behavior

Native receive/reconstruction/persistence work is separated and bounded; full source ordering, image identity, source clocks and withdrawal remain binding. The full-roster fast-codec trial still overflowed Kalshi and lost US transport. The installed alternative explicitly subscribes to **256 markets per source**, with half prioritized fairly across nearby league/family/event cells and half rotating the remaining catalog every **20 seconds**. The earlier 512/30 trial is retained below. Its expanded live population reached derived p95 14.48 seconds for Kalshi and 12.37 seconds for US, leaving too little of the 15-second window for capture/display; this motivated the smaller cohort. Discovery still retains the complete agreed scope. No memory, byte, hourly connection, freshness or paid-polling guard was increased.

At the 21:30 installed sample, Kalshi had 7,817 eligible markets (7,305 deferred), and US had 9,524 (9,012 deferred). Each had made 12 connections with zero error retries; Kalshi had 10 planned rotations and one catalog reconciliation, US 11 planned rotations. This is bounded, partial coverage. Nominal stable-roster revisit is about 15–19 minutes at these populations, plus connection cleanup and catalog delays. It is not continuous whole-slate freshness. Unchanged active cohorts survive changes confined to deferred catalog members; changed active material still requires resynchronization. Raw synchronization for an exact current-generation image is distinct from overall partial coverage and price freshness.

Publication now tracks exact captured aggregate receipts, including reference-only captures, separately from finance visits. It reserves oldest work and both native sources, and lets native slots advance an undelivered aggregate game when both needs overlap. Each component binds only to its actual sources. Optional finance shares the overall turn budget. These changes retain complete matching components, fair competing work and pinned immutable inputs.

The 21:30 check still exposed a long tail. A live SQL observation showed the publication lock held by a read-only `pgstattuple` scan. Maintenance now scans without that lock and acquires it only for an eligible physical rewrite, releasing it immediately after each rewrite attempt or headroom refusal and before recovery packing; copy runway, lock timeout, transactional rewrite, retention and pinned evidence guards remain. Across 285 subsequent installed lock samples through 22:23, none observed `pgstattuple` holding that lock; 49 observed physical rewrites and 193 publication/other holders. This is sampled evidence, alongside the targeted lock tests, not proof of every interleaving.

Exact matching includes source-scoped Sam Houston State and Jacksonville State college aliases. Native NO/not-win and Long/Short interpretations remain separate. Explicit source sporting state now passes the former pregame-only discovery gate. Live prices require a post-start source clock and expire after 15 seconds; market open/trading status alone never establishes a live game. Suspended/final images withdraw current prices; resumed active images must re-establish valid evidence.
