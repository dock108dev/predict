# Watching opportunities in Predict

Open **Advanced Scanner** in the page footer, or visit `/scanner`, for the
globally ranked Scanner and manual tickets described below. The landing page `/`
is the opportunity dashboard, with separate EV and arbitrage sections; `/arbs`
is the stored opposing-comparison view. `/odds` shows observed source prices and
`/ev` shows single-trade estimates. Use Admin to inspect source status and control
collection. None of these views places orders.

## Filters and observations

Choose supported venues, sports and market filters. Configured supported scope
can include NFL, NCAAF, NBA, NCAAB, MLB and NHL full-game winners, spreads and
totals. Available filter choices do not prove observed prices or qualified finance.
Both execution positions must
use included venues. **Find on this page** searches only the displayed page; it
does not narrow observation history or arrival cues. Amount is optional and does
not change discovery rows.

Open **Scope, size & observations** for distinct observed episodes in your selected
category and filters. “Newly qualified” requires an observed prior check without
that category. “Present at first observation” has unknown earlier history. A gap,
expiry, Stop or restart leaves history incomplete. A checked current zero differs
from unknown coverage and from opportunities observed earlier.

Edge and supported funding ranges describe observed allocations; their maxima may
occur at different times. When retained joint observations cannot establish both
minimum filters, historical threshold matches are labeled incomplete. First-to-last
observed spans do not establish uninterrupted market lifetime or reliable frequency.
History retains up to seven days, 10,000 episodes or 10 MiB; eviction and gaps are
disclosed.

## Inspect a manual ticket

1. Select a row and read its category. A conditional hedge can lose in exceptional
   outcomes; EV is an expectation. Inspect both native actions, quantities, original
   prices and clocks, funding, requested/deployed/residual cash and the buffer.
2. Change Amount to check a separate replacement. The selected calculation stays
   intact. Review changed allocations before choosing **Adopt updated allocations**.
3. Changed prices/depth, expiry, Stop or connection/context failure withdraw action
   authority. Status reads never renew original prices. Close or Escape restores
   scanner focus; updates preserve the Amount draft and disclosures.
4. Identity/search-text copying copies identification only. American odds are a
   comparison, never native order limits. Allocation copying and exact-market links
   require current ticket readiness and qualified destinations.

The production destination catalog is empty, so those execution aids remain
unavailable. No ticket reserves liquidity or submits an order. Filtering, sizing,
adoption and copying do not request source data or change a provider account.

Sound starts off. Enable or mute it deliberately. Cues cover the selected server
filters, including rows beyond the current page. Page movement, Find, filter
changes, reloads, reconnects and unknown continuity are silent.
