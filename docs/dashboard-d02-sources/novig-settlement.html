How Settlement Works — Grading, Disputes, and Voided Trades

Updated over a week ago
Table of contents

Once a game ends, Novig grades all trades against official data sources and settles them automatically. Settlement typically occurs within 30 minutes of the event ending. Your updated balance is reflected in your Portfolio.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Novig settle trades?

Results are verified against official data sources after the event concludes. Trades are then graded (won, lost, voided, or pushed) accordingly. You can track the status of any trade in your Portfolio.

How long does settlement take?

Settlement typically occurs within 30 minutes of the event ending.

My trade seems graded incorrectly. What do I do?

Reach out through Live Chat. Provide your trade or order slip ID (or the event name and your selection if you don't have the ID), and explain what you believe the correct outcome should be. Make sure to connect to a human agent. Our team will review against official statistics and respond, usually within 24–48 hours.

What happens when official statistics are corrected after a game?

If an official stat correction is issued after a game has ended, Novig is unable to re-grade affected trades. Trades are graded based on the official statistics available at the time of grading, and outcomes will not be adjusted if a correction is issued afterward. Your account balance will not be affected by any subsequent stat corrections.

What is a voided trade?

A trade is voided if the event is cancelled, a player doesn't participate, or other conditions in the Market Rules apply. When a trade is voided, you will be paid out at the Fair Market Value. You can learn more about how that's determined here.
​
For most markets if a event is postponed and rescheduled within 48 hours the trades will still be considered active. Please be sure to consult the specific market rule if you are uncertain.

What is a push?

A push occurs when the result exactly matches the line, or when a player (in props markets) doesn't participate at any point during the event. As with voids, pushes are also settled at Fair Market Value.

Note: "X+" markets (for example "2+ 3-pointers") do not push when the result lands exactly on the number. See the next section.

How do "X+" markets settle?

Markets shown as "X+" (for example "2+ 3-pointers" or "25+ points") ask whether the player reaches X or more.

  • If the player's final stat is equal to or greater than X, the market settles Yes.

  • If the player's final stat is less than X, the market settles No.

  • Landing exactly on X is not a push. Exactly X counts as Yes.

Example: You trade No on "2+ 3-pointers." The player makes exactly 2. The market settles Yes, and the No position loses.

A simple way to read these markets: "2+" means "2 or more."

Where do I find sport-specific void and push conditions?

These details are outlined in the contract for the specific market you've traded on. You can view all of our current contracts here.

Where can I see all my trade details?

In the Portfolio section of the app. Each trade shows the event, your selection, the odds, and the settlement result.
