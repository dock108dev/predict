| Signal | Meaning | Economic result |
|---|---|---|
| Arbitrage | Complementary prediction-market contracts assessed across material settlement outcomes. | Modeled worst-case net profit/ROI, conditional on specified fills and compatible settlement. |
| Mispricing | A venue price compared with our target-specific estimated fair price. | Net expected profit/return with model and outcome assumptions. |
| Lead/lag | Reference movement followed by delayed target response. | Observed movement signal; modeled net trade value only where evidence supports it. |
| Maker value | Proposed resting orders evaluated if filled. | Conditional net expected value; fill likelihood, adverse selection and inventory exposure remain separate. |
